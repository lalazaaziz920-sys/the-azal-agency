AZAL — EMBLEM HARDWARE

Metal flower emblem · hardware spec · v2
Notes for hardware vendor
  • Use the physical AZAL emblem as master (flower head + curved stem).
  • Back of the piece: shank/eyelet for sewn buttons and snaps, hook for clasps, cord hole or cord clamp for toggles.
  • Zinc alloy or brass, nickel-free, silver/rhodium plating, lacquer sealed.
  • Weight under 3 g (20 mm) so thin fabric does not pull; add fusing patch under every piece.
  • Snaps on LINE must survive washing: test 10 wash cycles.
  • Sizes: 20, 15 and 12 mm, two finishes.

Fabric rules
  • Crisp slub (WRAP, LINE): structured collar, strips, pocket flap. Fuse collar, cuffs, shoulder strip.
  • Soft smooth (LAYER, TOGGLE and all trousers): drape and flow.
  • Pre-wash a swatch, allow 3% shrinkage on length.
  • Dark colours: rub test before bulk cut.
Sampling plan
  • One prototype per style, size M. Photos front, back, side.
  • Test hardware on the thinnest fabric (LAYER vest, TOGGLE).
  • Confirm length: kurta stops at the hip, not below.
  • Deviation from callouts needs written approval from AZAL.
