AZAL — EMBLEM HARDWARE

Metal flower emblem · hardware spec for the factory
Notes for hardware vendor
  • Use the physical AZAL emblem sample as the master (flower head + curved stem).
  • Add a shank/eyelet on the back for sewing (buttons) or a pin + keeper (brooches).
  • Zinc-alloy or brass, nickel-free, rhodium/silver plating; lacquer-sealed against tarnish.
  • Weight under 3 g for 20 mm to avoid pulling thin fabric; sew with reinforcement patch.
  • Two finishes: polished silver, oxidized dark silver.
  • Scales needed: 25, 20, 15, 12 mm + graded set 18-8 mm (7 sizes) for Noir sleeve chain.

Fabric rules
  • Crisp slub fabrics are used for kameez (collar, placket, pintucks hold shape).
  • Soft smooth fabrics are used for trousers and fluid kameez (Noir, Slate).
  • Check shrinkage: pre-wash a swatch, 3% allowance on length.
  • Slub fabrics: use fusing on collar band and cuffs, soft interlining only.
  • Dark shades: use dye-fastness test (rub test) before bulk cut.
Sampling plan
  • Make 1 prototype per style in size M, photograph front/back/side.
  • Check emblem weight and sewing on the thinnest fabric (Noir, Slate).
  • Send fit photos for review before the size set (S-XL).
  • Trims, buttonholes and embroidery placement are measured from the sketch callouts.
  • Any deviation from the callouts must be approved by AZAL in writing.
