AZAL — THE EMBLEM

One emblem, one place · v4
Rules for every style
  • One emblem per garment (TWO-FACE: one per side, same spot).
  • The emblem is never a button, zip puller or clasp.
  • All functional hardware is plain: matte silver snaps 12 mm, black coil zips, simple metal pullers.
  • Zinc alloy or brass, nickel-free, silver plating, lacquer sealed, wash and rub tested (10 cycles).
  • Do not iron directly over the emblem. Care label: turn inside out for washing.
  • Fix the position with a paper template (cut from this sheet) at sampling.
